Five products, one routine

Wash, moisturise, and a balm for the dry patches. Then sun, and bedtime.

  1. Soft Suds

    The wash

    A gentle wash built on plant-based sugar cleansers, with aloe, oat and New Zealand kumarahou. Sulphate-free.

  2. Lotion Potion

    The everyday moisturiser

    A light, fast-absorbing body lotion for the whole family.

  3. Calm Balm

    For the dry, cranky patches

    A rich balm for dry, sensitive and eczema-prone skin. Shea butter, avocado oil, mānuka honey and kawakawa. Dermatologically tested. Steroid-free.

  4. Bedtime Bestie

    For winding down

    A magnesium and glycerin mist for the bedtime routine — a calming ritual, not a treatment.

  5. Sun Balm

    For outside

    A mineral sunscreen with zinc oxide as the only active filter. Broad-spectrum SPF 50.
